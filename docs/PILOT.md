# Pilot playbook (2 bars)

Real bar names, contacts and numbers stay OUT of this public repo. Keep them in a private note
and enter bars through the admin API at runtime.

## What to ask each bar
- Count the door on pilot nights (Thursday to Saturday, about 9pm to close) with the free staff
  counter on any phone. One person at the door taps In and Out.
- Set the outside line size when it changes (5 options, one tap).
- Tell us the legal capacity (posted occupancy) and whether they sell a line skip pass and its price.
- Permission to show the bar's name in the app and in social posts.
- A contact for questions and a weekly 10 minute check in.

## What the bar gets
- A free, simple door counter and an accurate read on the night.
- A weekly summary: peak headcount, busiest hour, average wait, how students rated the line.
- Early partner status and visibility to students in the app and on social.
- Optional later: promote a perk for Crowded reporters (skip the line once a month, no cover early).

## What we need from you to set them up (private, not in git)
Bar name, address, map coordinates, capacity, timezone, pass price (or none), and the names of the
door staff who will count. Then:
1. Admin creates the bar and sets the pass price.
2. Admin creates one invite per door staff member (single use, expires in 3 days).
3. Staff sign in at the staff page with their phone, confirm 21+, enter the invite code.
4. Two minute training: In, Out, +5, set the line size, check "What students see".

## Agreement (for the lawyers to draft)
A short pilot letter, not a heavy contract: duration (for example 6 weeks), what each side
does, data use (we only show aggregates, no staff personal data is shared), right to stop any time,
no exclusivity, permission to use the name and logo. Ask the lawyers about compensating staff for
counting (pay from us, the bar, or neither).

## Pilot success criteria (after 4 weekends)
- Freshness: 80 percent or more of 15 minute intervals from 10pm to 1am have recent data.
- Accuracy: estimate within 5 minutes of real timers on average (admin accuracy metric).
- Staff counter active on at least 80 percent of pilot nights.
- At least 100 students signed up through the beta link, and repeat use on 2 or more weekends.
- Bars want to continue.

## Weekly loop
Thursday: check both counters work. Friday and Saturday: watch freshness live, ping ambassadors.
Sunday: review accuracy, staff reliability, disagreement flags, and student feedback. Adjust.
