# Questions for counsel

A working list for the owner's lawyer friends. Grouped by urgency. The questions are generic on
purpose: real bar names and terms stay out of this public repo.

## Now (before the pilot)
1. **Entity.** LLC or something else, and in which state? Who are the members?
2. **Founder agreement.** Equity, vesting, roles, decision making, and what happens if someone
   leaves. Everyone who contributes (code, design, social, legal) signs an IP assignment.
3. **Trademark.** Is "Crowded" available for a mobile app (USPTO classes 9 and 42)? Any conflict
   with existing nightlife or crowd apps? Should we file now?
4. **Privacy Policy and Terms.** Review the drafts (site/privacy.html, site/terms.html). Which laws
   apply: Pennsylvania, other states where users live (California, Virginia, Colorado and others),
   breach notification rules? Is "location used once and discarded, only near or not near kept"
   described accurately? Do our App Store privacy answers (docs/APP_STORE.md) match?
5. **Age gate.** Is a self attested 21+ checkbox enough for an app about bars, or do we need ID
   verification? What is our exposure if someone under 21 uses it?
6. **Estimates and verdicts.** Disclaimer wording for wait estimates and "get the pass" advice. Any
   liability if an estimate is wrong and someone buys a pass or waits in the cold?
7. **Pilot letter with bars.** A short agreement covering duration, what each side does, who owns
   the door counts and specials data, confidentiality, use of the bar's name and logo, no
   exclusivity, termination, and a liability cap.
8. **Paying door staff and student ambassadors.** Independent contractors (1099)? Does paying a
   bar's employee a stipend create problems with their employer? Do ambassadors who post on social
   need "#ad" style disclosures (FTC)?

## Before showing real drink specials
9. **Pennsylvania happy hour and price advertising rules.** Can a third party app show a licensee's
   drink specials and happy hour times and prices? Does that make us an advertiser under the
   Liquor Code or PLCB rules? Are we liable if a bar posts a special that breaks the rules?
10. **Inducements and tied house rules.** Can bars pay us for featured placement or promotions? Can
    rewards include free cover or a line skip? We plan to never offer alcohol as a reward. Is that
    enough?
11. **Dram shop and social host exposure.** Any risk from promoting bars, deals or bar crawls?
12. **Menus and content.** Can we enter a bar's public menu ourselves with permission? Is written
    permission from each bar enough?

## Before selling cover or passes in the app
13. **Payments.** Do we need money transmitter licensing, or does a marketplace processor (for
    example Stripe Connect) cover it? Who is the merchant of record?
14. **Taxes.** Sales or amusement tax on cover charges and passes in Pennsylvania and the town.
15. **Refunds.** Terms for when a buyer is refused entry (ID, dress code, capacity), plus
    chargebacks and fraud.
16. **Apple.** Confirm that passes for entry to a physical venue can use outside payment (App Store
    guideline 3.1.3(e) and 3.1.5) and not in-app purchase.
17. **Competing with Line Leap.** If a bar has a contract with Line Leap (exclusivity, non compete),
    what is our exposure if that bar works with us, for example tortious interference? Do any Line
    Leap patents cover virtual line skip or entry passes? Get a freedom to operate check before we
    build payments.

## Marketing
18. **Raffles and giveaways.** Sweepstakes rules for weekly prize drawings (no purchase necessary,
    Pennsylvania rules, official rules page).
19. **Using bar names, logos and photos** in social posts. Is written permission needed?
20. **College and university rules.** Any issue with using the university name or campus
    references in marketing?
