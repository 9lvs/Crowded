Support

Email support@crowded.example. A real person reads every message. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Delete your account

  1. Open Crowded and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account, sessions, reports, timers and points are deleted right away and cannot be recovered. If you can't sign in anymore, email us from any address with the last 2 digits of your phone number and we will help.

Common questions

My login code never arrived

Codes can take up to a minute. Check the number you typed. You can ask for a new code after a short wait. After too many wrong codes, sign in pauses for 15 minutes to protect your account.

Why does a bar say "No recent data"?

Nobody has reported from that bar in the last 30 minutes, so we would rather show nothing than an old number. If you're there, a quick crowd report fixes it for everyone.

The wait was way off

Start a line timer next time ("I'm in line", then "I'm inside"). Real waits are the best way we have to correct the estimate. If something looks badly wrong, email us the bar and the time.

How do I change when the app recommends a pass?

In Settings, set your wait limit. When a bar sells a line skip pass and the wait is over your limit, we say so.

I work at a bar. How do I use the door counter?

Ask your manager for an invite code, open the staff counter, sign in with your phone, and enter the code. Each staff account works for one bar only.

Do you track my location?

No. Location is read once when you report or start a timer, used to check whether you are near the bar, and discarded. See the privacy policy.

Report a problem or abuse

If you think someone is sending fake reports, or you found a security issue, email support@crowded.example with as much detail as you can. For security issues, please don't share details publicly until we've had a chance to fix them.
