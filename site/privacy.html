Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE TO BE SET]. Last updated: [DATE].

Crowded ("we", "us") shows live line waits and crowd levels at bars, based on reports from students and counts from door staff. We built Crowded to collect as little about you as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your choices.

The short version

  • We never store your location or a history of where you have been.
  • We never store your phone number in readable form or your date of birth.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third party analytics or ad trackers.
  • Deleting your account in the app deletes everything tied to you.

Location

Crowded asks for your location only at the moment you send a crowd report or start a line timer, and only while you are using the app. We never track your location in the background.

When you do share it, the app sends your coordinates once. Our server works out how far you are from that bar, keeps only whether you were near it and a rough distance band (at the door, nearby, or far), and discards the coordinates. Coordinates are never written to our database or our logs. You can say no to location and still use Crowded. Your reports then count for a little less.

Staff accounts

Bar staff who use the door counter sign in the same way. We record the door counts and line updates they make, with their account and the time, so changes can be traced. Staff can only see and change the bar they were invited to.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. We permanently delete your account and everything linked to it: sessions, devices, reports, timers, settings and points. Bar level history that never contained your ID is not affected. Door counts made by a staff account stay, without the link to that account.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Identifiers are stored as keyed hashes. Access to production systems is limited and staff and admin actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Age

Crowded is only for people 21 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 21 to use it. If you believe someone under 21 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
