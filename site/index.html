How it works

The people in line are the data.

Crowded runs on quick taps from students who are already out, plus counts from door staff. The more people tap, the better the number gets for everyone.

  1. Check Tonight

    See the wait and how packed each bar is before you leave. Tap a bar for the normal wait at this hour and how full it is versus capacity.

  2. Tap when you're in line

    Hit "I'm in line", then "I'm inside" when you get in. Or send a two tap crowd report: how busy it is and how long the line looks.

  3. Everyone gets a better number

    Your real wait tunes the estimate for the next person. Door counts keep it honest when the bar is at capacity and only lets people in as others leave.

For bars

A free door counter that works on any phone.

Your door staff tap in and out. You get a live read on the night, and students get an honest picture of the line before they walk over.

Running a bar in our pilot town? partners@crowded.example

  • Nothing to install The counter is a web page. Staff sign in with a code from you.
  • Headcount versus capacity Big plus and minus buttons, plus the outside line size in one tap.
  • See what students report Crowd reports for your bar show up next to your own count.
  • One bar per account Staff only ever see and change their own bar. Every change is logged.

Privacy

We don't track where you go.

  • No location trail Your location is checked once, only when you report or start a timer, to see if you are near the bar. We keep "near" or "not near" and throw the rest away.
  • No birthday You confirm you're 21 or older. We store that you confirmed, nothing else.
  • Your number stays hidden We store a scrambled version of your phone number, never the number itself.
  • Delete means delete Delete your account in Settings and everything tied to you is gone.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Crowded is free for students and the door counter is free for bars.

Which bars?

We are starting with a small pilot of a few bars in one college town. More bars come once the numbers prove themselves.

Is it accurate?

It is an estimate, and it gets better as more people tap. We combine door counts, line timers and crowd reports, weigh recent and reliable reports more, and check our estimates against real waits every night. If there is not enough recent data, we show "No recent data" instead of a guess.

What do you do with my location?

Only when you report or start a line timer, the app reads your location once. Our server works out how far you are from that bar, keeps a simple "near" or "not near", and discards the coordinates. We never track you in the background and never store where you have been.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete account. Your account, reports, timers and points are deleted right away. See the privacy policy.
