CrowdedFor bars

Know your night.
Let students know your line.

Crowded shows college students the live line and crowd at bars downtown. We are piloting with two bars this season. Door staff use their own phone. No new hardware.

What you get

  • A free door counterAny phone at the door. Count people in with one tap. Out is optional.
  • A live read on your nightEntries, the line, how busy it is inside and the drink wait. Tonight so far, on your phone.
  • Your specials in front of studentsPost specials and happy hours once a week as a simple price list.
  • A weekly summaryEntries by hour, busiest hour, average wait and drink wait trend.

What we ask

  • Count the door on pilot nightsAbout 2 minutes of training. A cheap Bluetooth clicker works too.
  • Set the line and report insideDoor staff set the line size, then tap how busy it is inside and the drink wait. A reminder comes every 15 min.
  • Post your specials weeklyManagers do it once a week. You can choose to count entries only.
  • Name one contactFree for [6] weeks. Founding partner pricing after the pilot.

Privacy

We never sell your data. Paid promotions are always labeled and never change wait or crowd numbers. We never store a location trail. Your staff use their own phone with a single-use invite, and you can remove them any time.
