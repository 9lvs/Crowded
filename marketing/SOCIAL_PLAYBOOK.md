# Social playbook

For the founder and the social media and PR lead. Start next week with the Week 1 steps in the
launch arc. Creator program: see [CAMPUS_CREATORS.md](CAMPUS_CREATORS.md). Existing assets and
captions: [README.md](README.md) and [COPY.md](COPY.md). This playbook builds on them.

Placeholders in [brackets] are unknowns. Bar names in examples are FAKE (FAKE Owl, FAKE Anchor,
FAKE Barrel). Use a real bar only with that bar's written OK.

## Positioning in one line
**Know the line before you go.** Live bar lines, crowds and drink waits in [Town], from the
people standing in them. 21+.

## Voice
- Plain and a little dry. Say the useful thing, then stop.
- Short sentences. No em dashes, no semicolons, no hype words.
- A helpful friend, not a promoter. We save people time. We do not sell a good time.
- Never preachy. No lectures. A short "21+" and a ride home reminder when it fits is enough.
- Never "get wasted" culture. No drinking games, no "blackout", no counting drinks, no shots.
  Our subject is lines, crowds and waits, not intoxication.
- Honest numbers only. Every number shows its basis. If there is no recent data, the post says
  "No recent data". Never round up a story.
- Black and white visuals, same as the grid. Plain text on black or white. Screen recordings of
  the real app. Demo data is labeled FAKE.

Sample lines in voice:
- "FAKE Owl: 25 min line. FAKE Anchor: none. Walk between them: 4 min."
- "Nobody has reported in 30 minutes. We say so."
- "Two taps. Everyone behind you thanks you."

## Content pillars
| Pillar | What it is | Share of posts | Main data |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Line Report | Live and weekly lines, forecasts, drink waits | 35 percent | Estimates, typical-hour history, aggregate weekend stats |
| 2. Deals tonight | Specials that bars post to us | 20 percent | Bar posted deals (bar OK needed) |
| 3. Know before you go | Practical tips for a night out. Cover, ID, timing, rides | 20 percent | App data plus common sense |
| 4. Behind the door | Door staff and managers, with permission | 10 percent | Interviews, door counter screens |
| 5. Campus nights | Culture, memes, student voices | 10 percent | Creators, polls, UGC |
| 6. How it works and trust | Basis lines, privacy, 21+, "No recent data" | 5 percent | Existing grid posts, screen recordings |

Shares are a starting guess, not a target. Adjust after 4 weeks of metrics.

Pillar 2 stays off until the pilot bars agree and counsel has answered the happy hour and
inducement questions (docs/LEGAL_QUESTIONS.md items 9 and 10). Until then, put its share into
pillars 1 and 3.

Rules for all pillars:
- Paid promotions are always labeled "Promoted". A paid post never changes a wait or crowd number.
- No identifiable people without consent. No minors. No one visibly drinking in any post.
- Tag the area or [Town], not a specific bar, unless that bar is a partner and said yes.

## Post and Reel ideas
Each idea lists the hook, the format, when to post and what it needs. Times are local. Where an
idea says "typical-hour data", we need enough real weeks of history first. If we do not have it,
skip the idea or use the "No recent data" angle.

### Pillar 1: The Line Report
**1. Line or no line? (story poll)**
- Hook: "Thursday, 9 pm. Is there a line? Tap one."
- Format: story poll with two options, "Line" and "No line". Add a link sticker.
- When: Thursday 9 pm. Post results Friday morning.
- Needs: story-2 template, poll sticker. Compare to the app only if the app had recent data.

**2. Friday line forecast (carousel)**
- Hook: "When is the line shortest at FAKE Owl? Friday forecast."
- Format: 4 slides. Title. Usual line by hour for one bar. The best window. "Based on [N]
  reports over [N] weeks" with the link.
- When: Friday 3 pm.
- Needs: typical-hour data for a bar (the hour chart already exists). Basis on every slide.
  Skip any bar with too little data.

**3. Drink wait of the week (feed post or story)**
- Hook: "Longest wait for a drink last weekend: [N] minutes. Shortest: [N]."
- Format: one black card, big number, basis line.
- When: Monday 12 pm.
- Needs: drink wait readings (staff and student). Bar names only with OK. Otherwise say
  "bar A" and "bar B".

**4. The 30 second walk (Reel)**
- Hook: "Two bars. 30 seconds apart. One has a 25 minute line. One has none."
- Format: 10 to 15 second Reel. Screen recording of the Tonight list, then a point of view walk
  with no faces. End card: "Know the line before you go."
- When: Thursday or Friday 8:30 pm, before the night starts.
- Needs: live data at post time with a visible timestamp, or demo data (`?mock=1`) labeled FAKE.
  Real bar names only with OK.

**5. Weekend recap, aggregate only (feed post)**
- Hook: "Last weekend in [Town]: busiest hour was [11 pm]. Shortest average line was [time]."
- Format: one stat card plus a small hour chart.
- When: Sunday 6 pm.
- Needs: aggregate stats from the app. Never single users, never anyone's movements. If we have
  fewer than [N] reports, post "Quiet weekend for reports. Thank you to everyone who tapped."

**6. The 10:30 crunch (static chart)**
- Hook: "The 10:30 pm crunch, in one chart."
- Format: single image, line chart, one bar, 3 nights overlaid.
- When: Wednesday 4 pm.
- Needs: typical-hour data. Basis line.

**7. "I'm in line" timer challenge (Reel, UGC)**
- Hook: "Start the timer when you join the line. Stop it when you're in. Post your number."
- Format: screen recording of the timer from "I'm in line" to "I'm inside" with the real minutes
  on screen. 7 to 15 seconds. Caption: "Real wait: [N] min".
- When: Thursday to Saturday, posted later that night or next day.
- Needs: timer screen, a clean overlay template, a hashtag [#crowdedtimer]. Film the phone screen
  only, not other people in line. Never film while walking across a street or driving.

**8. Guess the wait (story quiz)**
- Hook: "How long was the line at FAKE Owl at 10:15 last Saturday?"
- Format: quiz sticker with 4 answers. Reveal the real timers in a second story.
- When: Tuesday 7 pm.
- Needs: completed timer data from a past night. The reveal says "Based on [N] timers".

**9. No recent data day (feed post)**
- Hook: "Quiet Tuesday. Nobody has reported, so we show nothing. That is the point."
- Format: black card with "No recent data" in large type.
- When: Tuesday 8 pm on a slow night.
- Needs: the real empty state screenshot. It works because it is honest.

### Pillar 2: Deals tonight
**10. Deals tonight (story, pilot nights)**
- Hook: "Tonight: [no cover before 10 at FAKE Barrel]."
- Format: text story on black, one deal each, one link sticker. Max 3 deals.
- When: 5 pm Thursday to Saturday.
- Needs: deals posted by the bar's manager in the app, plus that bar's OK. Cover, entry and food
  deals first. No drink prices or drink specials until counsel clears them. Label "Promoted"
  when a bar pays.

**11. Cheapest way in (carousel)**
- Hook: "Four ways to skip full cover this weekend."
- Format: 5 slides, one offer per slide with bar, time window and rule.
- When: Thursday 12 pm.
- Needs: partner bar offers (cover or entry), each approved by the bar before posting.

**12. Is the pass worth it? (Reel)**
- Hook: "25 minute line. $[ ] pass. Worth it? Here is how the app decides."
- Format: screen recording of the verdict screen. Explain the default 20 minute threshold and
  that users can change it.
- When: Friday 6 pm.
- Needs: a bar with a manually entered pass price and its OK. Basis line visible. No savings
  claims beyond what the app shows.

### Pillar 3: Know before you go
**13. Checklist (carousel, built to be saved)**
- Hook: "Before you leave: ID, charged phone, a ride home plan."
- Format: 5 slide checklist. Last slide shows how to check the line in the app.
- When: Thursday 5 pm. Repost at the start of each semester.
- Needs: no data. Keep the ride line plain, not preachy.

**14. Cold night math (card or 8 second Reel)**
- Hook: "It is [28 F]. A 25 minute line feels longer. Check first."
- Format: single card or text Reel.
- When: any cold night, 7 pm.
- Needs: current line data. With no recent data, drop the number and keep the tip.

**15. Best time to go (story series)**
- Hook: "Want a short line? Go before [10 pm] or after [12:30 am]."
- Format: 3 stories, one bar each, one sentence each.
- When: Saturday 4 pm.
- Needs: typical-hour data and basis. Skip when data is thin.

**16. New student guide (carousel)**
- Hook: "First week in [Town]? Read this before your first Thursday."
- Format: 6 slides. 21+ reminder, how lines work downtown, how to use the app.
- When: the week before classes and the first week of each semester.
- Needs: copy from COPY.md. One plain line telling under 21 students the app is not for them yet.

### Pillar 4: Behind the door
**17. Staff POV: 200 people at the door (Reel)**
- Hook: "What it looks like from the door at 11 pm."
- Format: 15 to 20 second Reel over the shoulder of a door counter, tapping the staff screen.
  Faces out of frame or blurred. Text: "One tap per person. That is the data."
- When: Friday 11:30 pm, or Saturday morning.
- Needs: written OK from the bar and the staff member. No faces without consent. Check that
  nobody visibly drunk is in the shot. Replace "200" with the real count only if true.

**18. Meet the door (feed post)**
- Hook: "[First name] has worked the door at [bar] for [N] years. Three things they know."
- Format: portrait card plus a 3 line quote.
- When: Wednesday 12 pm.
- Needs: written OK from the person and the bar. They approve the quote before posting.

**19. Door counter tour (carousel)**
- Hook: "No hardware. Any phone. Here is how a bar uses Crowded."
- Format: 5 slides of the staff screen. Counter, line, crowd level, drink wait.
- When: Tuesday 1 pm. Also send it to prospective bars.
- Needs: FAKE data screenshots from `/staff?mock=1`, or real ones with the bar's OK.

### Pillar 5: Campus nights
**20. The black card meme (static, recurring series)**
- Hook: one line, white text on black. Examples: "The line was 5 minutes. It felt like 40."
  "Everyone says 'two minutes'." "Group chat, 10:45: where are we going."
- Format: single card in the existing type system. Same layout every time, so it reads as a
  series.
- When: Wednesday and Saturday afternoons.
- Needs: a good line. Humor about lines and plans, never about drinking.

**21. Plans vs reality (Reel)**
- Hook: "Plan: leave at 9. Reality: leaving at 10:40."
- Format: 7 second text-only Reel on black, typewriter effect, ends on the app name.
- When: Thursday 7 pm.
- Needs: text only. No people. Easy to produce in bulk.

**22. Send this to your friend (story template, UGC)**
- Hook: "Send this to the friend who says 'there's no line'."
- Format: story with a link sticker. Fans reshare with their own caption.
- When: Friday 8 pm.
- Needs: a story template. Use a made up chat on our own card. Never show real private chats.

**23. Campus org spotlight (feed post, UGC)**
- Hook: "[Club] tested the line timer this weekend. Here is what they found."
- Format: a quote card, or one photo of the group at a non-bar place (all 21+ and consenting,
  no alcohol in frame).
- When: Monday 5 pm.
- Needs: written OK from the org. No university logos or marks.

### Pillar 6: How it works and trust
**24. Behind the number (Reel)**
- Hook: "How we got 'About 25 min'. Seven reports and one staff count."
- Format: 20 second explainer. Screen recording of the basis line, then a simple diagram.
- When: Tuesday 12 pm.
- Needs: a real estimate with its basis. Explain the weighting in one plain sentence. Staff
  counts count for more, and reports from people near the bar count for more.

**25. We say how far off we were (feed post)**
- Hook: "Our estimates were off by [N] minutes on average last weekend."
- Format: stat card with basis and one line on what we are fixing.
- When: Monday, after [4] pilot weekends.
- Needs: the accuracy number from the admin metrics, posted as measured. Never cherry pick.

**26. Privacy in 10 seconds (Reel)**
- Hook: "We never store where you go."
- Format: 10 second text Reel on black. "Location checked once. Then dropped. No trail. No birthday."
- When: Wednesday 7 pm. Also pin it.
- Needs: existing copy from post 5. Counsel reviews the privacy wording first.

**27. FAQ highlight**
- Hook: "Is it free? Is it 21+? Do you track me?"
- Format: highlight cover plus 5 story cards.
- When: set up once, update monthly.
- Needs: existing highlight covers. Answers from the privacy page.

## Weekly cadence
Nights that matter are Thursday to Saturday. Post the day before and story during the night.

| Day | Feed | Stories | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Weekend recap or drink wait of the week (3, 5) | Repost the best fan story | Recap only with enough data |
| Tue | Trust or explainer (24, 9, 19) | Guess the wait quiz (8) | Slow day. Teach |
| Wed | Meme card or Meet the door (20, 18) | Poll about weekend plans | Light |
| Thu | Know before you go (13) | 5 pm Deals (10). 9 pm Line or no line (1). 11 pm repost fans | First big night |
| Fri | Forecast carousel or Reel (2, 4) | 5 pm Deals. 9 pm line story with link. 10:30 pm poll results | Biggest night. Keep it short |
| Sat | Meme or Plans vs reality (20, 21) | 5 pm Deals. 10 pm line story. Close Friends check in | Reshare fan posts |
| Sun | Rest, or one thank you post | Thank the reporters (names only with OK) | Answer DMs and comments |

Workload: 4 or 5 feed posts and 1 or 2 Reels a week, plus Thursday to Saturday stories. Realistic
for one person.

Do not post after midnight on pilot nights. Never tell people to go out or stay out. Our job is
the line, not the night.

## Stories and interactive features
- **Polls.** Two options, about lines or plans, never about drinking. Thursday: "Line or no line?"
  Friday: "Which bar had the shortest line?" Report the result next to the app's number.
- **Quizzes.** "Guess the wait" with a real number from a past night. Reveal in the next story
  with "Based on [N] timers".
- **Countdown stickers.** Beta start, first bar live, first big weekend. Turn on reminders.
- **Link stickers.** TestFlight link once it exists. Put the sticker above the arrow and outside
  the top 250 px and bottom 340 px (see COPY.md). Use a separate link per creator if we have a
  tracked link (see CAMPUS_CREATORS.md).
- **Close Friends.** Use it for beta testers. Good for new build notes, "report from the line
  tonight" asks, early screenshots and feedback. Never put a bar's name or deal there before the
  bar agrees. Close Friends is not private. Treat everything as public.
- **Question stickers.** "What would you want to know before you leave the house?" Answer the
  best ones in stories. Never answer with anyone's identity or movements.
- **Highlights.** How it works, Privacy, Bars, Beta, FAQ.
- **Age gate.** If a post touches alcohol at all, set the 21+ age restriction where the platform
  allows it. Prefer not touching alcohol.

## TikTok and Threads
**TikTok.** 7 to 15 second vertical clips, hook in the first second. Reuse ideas 4, 7, 17, 21 and
26. Shorter captions, 2 or 3 hashtags, text on screen for sound off. Same black and white look.
TikTok has strict alcohol rules, so show none. Check TikTok's current rules for branded content
(use the Branded Content toggle for creator posts).

**Threads.** Text first. One line, one number, link in a reply. Examples:
- "FAKE Owl line: none. FAKE Anchor: 25 min. Based on [N] reports. [link]"
- "Nobody reported in 30 minutes, so the app says 'No recent data'. We prefer that to a guess."
- Thursday 9 pm: "Line or no line? Reply with your bar and your wait."

Do not quote people's replies without asking. Do not tag real bars without OK. Same handle on
all three (see handle list in COPY.md).

## Metrics that matter
Review weekly on Monday. Group 1 decides what we do next. Group 2 is context.

**Group 1: does content turn into use**
| Metric | How we get it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Installs from link clicks | Link clicks by source against TestFlight or App Store installs. No tracking pixels (see CAMPUS_CREATORS.md) | Is content turning into users |
| Beta signups | TestFlight joins, waitlist form, DMs. One row per source | Top of funnel |
| Reports per user per night | Aggregate from the app (reports and timers per 100 active users per night, see SOURCING.md) | Content should lead to taps |
| Share of peak intervals with recent data | Existing freshness metric (target 80 percent or more) | Social should help coverage |
| Joined Thursday, back by next Saturday | Aggregate cohort count | Is it a habit |

**Group 2: reach and trust (second)**
- Reel and story views, saves and shares. Saves and shares beat likes.
- Followers, only as context.
- Comments that ask a question. Count them and answer them.
- Problems reported: wrong numbers, rude comments, an unhappy bar. Answer within [24] hours.

**How to read it.** Many views and no clicks is entertainment. Few views and many installs is the
post to repeat. Keep one sheet: idea number, date, link clicks, signups, reports that night. One
night proves nothing, so look at 3 weeks.

**No invented stats.** Every figure we post comes from the app's data or a count we can show.

## Brand and safety rules for every post
- 21+ in the audience, in the copy and on camera.
- No excessive drinking, no drinking games, no drunk driving.
- No identifiable person without consent. No minors.
- Bar permission before filming inside. No real bar names or logos without written OK.
- No university names, logos or marks.
- No live locations of individuals. Post about a night after it happens, or at bar level.
- Follow Instagram and Meta's branded content and alcohol policies. Age-gate alcohol related
  content to 21+ where the platform allows it.
- Creator content carries the disclosure (see CAMPUS_CREATORS.md).
- Open legal questions are in docs/LEGAL_QUESTIONS.md, especially items 9, 10 and 18 to 20.
