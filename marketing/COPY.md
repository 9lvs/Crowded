# Crowded social copy

Rules for every post: short plain sentences. No em dashes, no semicolons. No invented stats,
users or reviews. Say 21+ often. Replace [Town], [date], [link] and bar names before posting.

## Handle ideas (check availability, @crowded is likely taken)
1. @crowded.app
2. @getcrowded
3. @crowded[town] (for example @crowdedstatecollege)
4. @crowdedhq
5. @crowdedlines
6. @usecrowded

Pick one that also works on TikTok and Threads, so all three match.

## Bio options (150 characters max)
1. Know the line before you go. Live bar lines and crowds in [Town]. 21+ only. Beta below.
2. Live bar lines and crowds, from people in line right now. [Town]. 21+. Join the beta below.
3. Every number shows its source. Bar lines and crowds in [Town]. 21+. Free beta below.

## Link in bio
Instagram allows up to 5 links in the bio. Order matters. The first one shows.
1. **Primary: TestFlight public link** (iPhone beta). Label it "Join the iPhone beta".
   Needs the Apple Developer account and a Beta App Review first.
2. **Later: App Store link** replaces the TestFlight link at launch.
3. **Privacy Policy** once it is hosted. Apple requires a public URL for it anyway.
4. Optional: "For bars" link to a form or email.

Until the TestFlight link exists, use "Coming soon" posts (6a) and collect interest with
story polls and DMs. Do not post a dead link.

## Grid captions (post in reverse order so 1 ends up top left)
Post them as 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 so the grid reads 1 to 6 from the top.

**1. Know the line**
Know the line before you go. Crowded shows live lines and crowds at bars in [Town], from the
people standing in them. 21+.
#[town] #[school] #nightlife #collegelife

**2. How it works**
Check tonight. Report or time your line. Everyone gets a better number. Door staff count too.
The more people report, the sharper it gets.
#[town] #[school] #collegenightlife

**3. Every number shows its source**
We show what every number is based on. If nobody has reported in 30 minutes, we say "No recent
data". We don't guess.
#[town] #[school]

**4. Two taps**
Packed or not. Line or not. Two taps from the door and you just helped everyone behind you.
#[town] #[school]

**5. Privacy**
We never store where you go. Location is checked once when you report, to see if you're near
the bar. Then it's dropped. No trail. No birthday. Just 21+.
#[town] #privacy

**6a. Coming soon**
Coming soon to [Town]. Turn on post notifications and you'll know first. 21+.
#[town] #[school]

**6b. Now in beta**
Now in beta on iPhone. Free. Link in bio. 21+.
#[town] #[school]

**Location tag:** tag the downtown area or the city, not a specific bar, unless that bar is a
partner and has said yes.

## Carousel caption (line timer)
Just got in line? Tap "I'm in line". Got in? Tap "I'm inside". Your real wait makes the next
estimate better for everyone behind you. Link in bio.

## Story text
1. **Launch:** use story-1. Add a link sticker to the TestFlight link.
2. **Poll:** use story-2. Add the poll sticker in the empty space. Options: two real bar names
   (only partner bars, or generic "Bar A / Bar B" style if not). Repost results the next day.
3. **Countdown:** use story-3. Add the countdown sticker in the empty space with the beta date.
4. **Tap the link:** use story-4. Put the link sticker above the arrow.

Keep stickers and text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px. The PNGs already respect that.

## TikTok and X / Threads
- **TikTok:** 7 to 15 second vertical clips. Hook in the first second ("This bar has a 40 minute
  line. This one has none."). Screen record the Tonight list (demo data labeled) and a two tap
  report. Same captions, fewer hashtags.
- **X / Threads:** text first. "Know the line before you go. Live bar lines in [Town], from people
  in line. 21+. Beta: [link]". Reply threads with the "every number shows its source" idea.
